# Your trip: Bozeman to Missoula, by way of Yellowstone and Glacier

You and your partner live in Chicago. You're flying into Bozeman, Montana, renting a car, and taking a week to see Yellowstone and Glacier before you fly home from Missoula.

## What you know

These are your facts. Give them when the planner asks.

- **What the trip is about:** Yellowstone and Glacier National Parks, and the wide open country in between.
- **Start:** the Bozeman airport, where you pick up the rental car. "Bozeman airport" is enough.
- **End:** the Missoula airport, where you drop the car and fly home. "Missoula airport" is enough.
- **Start date:** Monday, September 13, 2027. Your flight lands in the morning, so you can start driving around 10 a.m. on Day 1.
- **Trip length:** 7 days. See change 4.
- **Route:** scenic is nice, but you don't mind the interstate on the long stretches.
- **Driving:** you're used to long drives. About 6 hours a day is comfortable. See change 1.
- **Start time:** 7 a.m. is fine most days. You like early starts for the wildlife.
- **Interests:** wildlife, geysers and hot springs, big mountain views, and short hikes. Old West history. Anything with huckleberries.
- **Must-sees:**
  - Old Faithful
  - Grand Prismatic Spring
  - Lamar Valley at dawn, for wolves and bison
  - Going-to-the-Sun Road in Glacier
  - Lake McDonald
- **Places you already want,** if the planner asks what you have in mind:
  - the Roosevelt Arch, at the north entrance in Gardiner
  - Virginia City, the old gold-rush town, if it fits on the way to Yellowstone
  - a huckleberry milkshake somewhere near Glacier
- **Meals:** casual and hearty. Breakfast early, since you start early. Dinner can be a bit nicer.
- **Lodging:** mid-range. You'd like a night inside a park, if there's one. See change 2.
- **Total budget:** you don't have one. If asked, say mid-range is fine.

## What you haven't decided

- Where to sleep each night. You're open to the planner's ideas. Two or three nights near Yellowstone and two near Glacier sounds right to you.
- Which hotels. Pick from the option cards.
- Other stops. You're open to suggestions.

## Changes to make along the way

Make each change at the point it says, in your own words.

1. **One long day.** You'd rather drive from Yellowstone to Glacier in one day, even if it's about 7 hours. If the planner asks about a long day, say it's fine for that one. If it splits the drive over two days, ask to do it in one.
2. **A night in the park.** When the hotels come up, ask to spend one night inside Yellowstone. Go with what the planner says. If it says there's nothing inside the park, take the nearest town.
3. **Wolves at dawn.** After the Yellowstone days are laid out, ask to be in Lamar Valley at sunrise. If that means a very early start, agree to it, but only for that day.
4. **A day shorter.** After the hotels are picked, say your flight home moved a day earlier, so the trip is now 6 days. Ask the planner to make it fit, and go with what it suggests to cut.
5. **A new session.** After changes 1 to 4 are done, start a new session on the trip with `./chat.sh new-session <trip ID>`. Ask what's left to decide, and settle whatever it says.

## When the trip is planned

The trip is planned when all of these are true:

- Every night has a hotel picked, and every stop has a place picked. `./chat.sh cards` shows no open option cards.
- All the changes above are done.
- The planner, asked what's left to decide, names nothing, or only things you're happy to leave as they are.

Then thank the planner and stop.

If the trip still isn't planned after 80 of your messages, stop anyway, and say in your final note what was still open.
