# Your trip: Grand Junction to Salt Lake City, by way of Moab and Capitol Reef

You live in Grand Junction, Colorado. You and your partner are taking five days to see the red rock country of southern Utah: a few days in Moab for the arches, then Capitol Reef, then Salt Lake City, where you'll fly home from.

## What you know

These are your facts. Give them when the planner asks.

- **What the trip is about:** the red rock parks of southern Utah, with hiking, arches, and dark skies.
- **Start:** your home in Grand Junction. "Grand Junction" is enough.
- **End:** Salt Lake City. "Salt Lake City" is enough.
- **Start date:** Sunday, October 10, 2027. See change 4.
- **Trip length:** 5 days. See change 5.
- **Where you sleep:** 3 nights in Moab, then 1 night in Torrey, near Capitol Reef. If the planner asks or suggests, that's what you want.
- **Route:** scenic. On Day 1, you want to drive along the Colorado River on Highway 128, by Dewey Bridge, not the interstate the whole way.
- **Driving:** about 4 hours of driving a day is comfortable. See change 3.
- **Start time:** 8 a.m. is fine on most days. Two days are different: Day 2 starts at 5:30 a.m. for a sunrise hike, and the last day starts at 10 a.m., after a late night of stargazing.
- **Interests:** hiking, the arches and the red rock, and the night sky. Good food, but nothing fancy.
- **Must-sees:**
  - Delicate Arch at sunrise, on Day 2
  - Capitol Reef
  - Goblin Valley, with its hoodoos, on the way from Moab to Torrey
  - stargazing at Capitol Reef, late on the night in Torrey, around 1 a.m.
- **Places you already want,** if the planner asks what you have in mind:
  - Fisher Towers, on the river road on Day 1
  - dinner at Moab Brewery, at 6 p.m. on Day 1
  - the Windows Section in Arches, after the sunrise hike on Day 2
  - breakfast at Love Muffin Cafe in Moab, after the hikes on Day 2
  - lunch at Milt's Stop & Eat, at noon on Day 2
  - the Corona Arch trail, in the afternoon on Day 2
  - dinner at Desert Bistro, at 7 p.m. on Day 2
  - a rest day on Day 3, with dinner at The Spoke on Center, at 6:30 p.m.
  - pie at Gifford Homestead in Capitol Reef, on Day 4
  - Capitol Gorge, at the end of the Scenic Drive in Capitol Reef, on Day 4
- **Meals:** casual, local places. You like a good breakfast.
- **Lodging:** mid-range for Moab, since you're there 3 nights. Value for the one night in Torrey.
- **Total budget:** you don't have one. If asked, say mid-range is fine, and cheap is fine for one night.

## What you haven't decided

- Where to have breakfast in Grand Junction before you leave on Day 1. Ask the planner to suggest a place.
- Which hotels. Pick from the option cards.
- Anything else on Day 4. You're open to suggestions.

## Changes to make along the way

Make each change at the point it says, in your own words.

1. **Milt's.** If the planner says Milt's is closed, or closes soon after you'd get there, ask to move it to the morning. If the planner never says so, skip this change.
2. **The pie.** If the planner says Gifford Homestead is closed when you'd get there, first say you want to keep it anyway. Then change your mind, and ask for somewhere else for pie. If it offers some, pick one. Later, after at least one other change, say you've forgotten which pie places it offered, and ask what they were. If the planner never says Gifford Homestead is closed, skip this change.
3. **A longer day.** If the planner says Day 4's drive is over what you're comfortable with, agree to the longer day, since you want to see the hoodoos and Capitol Reef on the way.
4. **A day later.** After the itinerary shows the weather and what to pack, say you have to leave a day later, on Monday, October 11. Keep everything else the same.
5. **One night less in Moab.** After change 4 is done, say you'd like only 2 nights in Moab, and accept that the trip gets a day shorter. Then ask for a 5-hour hike in Arches on the morning of Day 2. Deal with anything that change causes.
6. **A new session.** After changes 1 to 5 are done, start a new session on the trip with `./chat.sh new-session <trip ID>`. Ask "why did we pick that Moab hotel?" Then ask what's left to decide, and settle whatever it says.

## When the trip is planned

The trip is planned when all of these are true:

- Every night has a hotel picked, and every stop has a place picked. `./chat.sh cards` shows no open option cards.
- All the changes above are done, or skipped where they say to.
- The planner, asked what's left to decide, names nothing, or only things you're happy to leave as they are.

Then thank the planner and stop.

If the trip still isn't planned after 80 of your messages, stop anyway, and say in your final note what was still open.
